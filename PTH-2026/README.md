## Talk Abstract:

**Tokenmaxxing: What Observability Can Teach Us About AI**
It's hard to escape the constant headlines about AI and the latest metric everyone seems to be celebrating: tokens. Token usage has become the new shiny KPI, with companies proudly sharing adoption numbers and encouraging developers to use more AI, as if more tokens were a proxy for better engineering.

As observability engineers, we've seen this pattern before. We've spent years helping teams distinguish useful signals from misleading ones, and we've learned that metrics often lose their value once they become the goal.

In this talk, we'll use the idea of tokenmaxxing as a starting point to explore why engineering organizations keep chasing the wrong metrics, what observability teaches us about measuring complex systems, and how to evaluate AI adoption without falling into the trap of optimizing dashboards instead of outcomes. Because in the end, shipping better software matters a lot more than generating more tokens.
